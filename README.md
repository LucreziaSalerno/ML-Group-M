# ML-Group-M
Machine Learning Project

# Business Problem
Football clubs waste too much money on overpriced transfers. The global transfer market exceeds billions of euros, yet quite a few high-value signings fail to deliver within two years. Mid-tier clubs face the biggest challenge. They can’t afford expensive scouting networks, yet they need to manage their budget more carefully. Traditional scouting creates market inefficiencies where clubs overpay for hyped players while missing out on hidden gems. We need a data-driven approach to identify fair player values and uncover those hidden gems, the undervalued talent, before other clubs find them.

# Business Context
We are a sports analytics startup helping mid-tier football clubs (€50M-€200M budgets) compete with wealthier rivals through data-driven player recruitment. Our solution for this problem is an ML model that predicts fair market values for professional players based on performance, age, league quality, and contract situations. By identifying players whose statistical performance suggests they’re undervalued compared to the market price, we help clubs make smarter transfer decisions. Success, in our case, means that our model achieves a significant prediction accuracy of actual market values and delivers a ranked list of undervalued players for recruitment.
